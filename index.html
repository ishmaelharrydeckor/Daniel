About Me

Hello, I’m Daniel

Here you’ll find what I do and how I can support your energy business.

  • I support oil and gas business operations with research-backed recommendations, developing market research, industry reports and policy analyses for downstream petroleum companies.

  • I help service companies satisfy their clients by putting quality management systems in place and reducing operational downtime, with skills in process safety and accident investigation.

  • I help industry professionals strengthen their media presence through articles and social media, and serve as Communications Officer for SPE Africa Region.

  • I chair the Membership Committee of SPE Ghana Section, leading growth, engagement and retention for Ghana’s energy professionals.
